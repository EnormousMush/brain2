# What would make this worth publishing

Not "is it true" but "why does it need to exist." Reviewed whenever the draft's shape changes.

## The four levels, weakest to strongest

**Level 1 — Synthesis.** Scattered material in one place. *A magazine essay, not a paper. The draft is currently here.*

**Level 2 — A distinction.** Naming a difference that was being elided, such that arguments must be restated once it is on the table. **The outputs/capacity distinction (§4) is a level-2 candidate** — conditional on C8.

**Level 3 — A diagnosis.** Explaining *why* a state of affairs persists, via a mechanism that predicts something checkable. **§2 aims at level 3 and has not reached it** — it describes incentives; a diagnosis would say what the incentive structure predicts.

**Level 4 — A reframing.** Changing what the question is. Jonas: after him, "what should we do" became "what kind of act is this." Russell–Einstein: after them, "which side should win" became "shall the species continue." **Rare, and what a manifesto is for.**

**Honest current assessment (updated [phone]): a strong level 2, a level-3 diagnosis in progress — and now a genuine design proposal with two original components: the space-commons funding argument (property, not charity — C11) and the dual-pipe network (capacity and outputs at once). If S7 closes, the design itself is a contribution. The level-4 reframing remains available on top.**

## The level-4 candidate worth attacking

If "together" means **(2) — progress whose production everyone participates in** — the available reframing is:

> The question is not how to distribute what AI produces. It is whether a technology that concentrates the *capacity to produce* can leave humanity a collective agent at all — and if not, what "human progress" then names.

This connects distribution to agency (Kulveit et al., framed as loss; the constructive counterpart unwritten) and makes the essay about **what humanity is** rather than who gets what. **The level-4 move if there is one — and the most likely to overreach.** [Judgement needed.]

## The three fastest ways to raise the level

1. **Do the audit (C7).** Original evidence separates a paper from an essay. Bounded.
2. **Answer "AI as normal technology."** Seriously engaging it is doing philosophy; ignoring it is doing atmosphere.
3. **Put first-hand material in.** Nobody else in this space has worked inside research systems on both sides of the US–China divide. `4-sources/firsthand/` is empty and it is the cheapest source of things nobody else can say.

## Where the value is not

- Not in agreeing that benefits should be shared — state consensus, worth nothing.
- Not in a new set of principles.
- Not in an institutional design — see `9-archive/`.
