# Claims ledger

Every load-bearing claim in `DRAFT.md`, with its evidential status on its face. **A claim cannot appear in the draft as fact unless its row here says `supported`.**

Status vocabulary — identical everywhere in this vault:

- **`supported`** — I read the source. Cite-ready.
- **`reported`** — a credible secondary source says it; the primary was unreachable. Usable with attribution to the reporter, never as bare fact.
- **`contested`** — serious people disagree. Presented as contested or not at all.
- **`asserted`** — our opinion. Fine in argument, fatal as evidence.
- **`unverified`** — not checked. **Never enters the draft.**
