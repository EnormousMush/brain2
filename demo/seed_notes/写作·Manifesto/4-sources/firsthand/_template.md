# Short name for the friction

## What happened

Dates, people (by role, not name unless it matters), what was being attempted. Write it as an incident report, not a reflection.

## What exactly blocked it

The specific rule, system, incentive or person. Not "bureaucracy." Which form, which review, which clause, which absent person.

## What it cost

Weeks, dollars, a dropped question, a dataset that never got shared. Quantify it.

## The workaround, and what the workaround cost

## Which structure produced it

Funding, review, credit, employment, jurisdiction, language, tooling. If it is more than one, say which is load-bearing.

## Would the proposed institution have avoided it?

Be honest. Cases where it would not are the most valuable notes in this folder — they are the ones that force the design to change.

## Generalizable?

n=1 or a pattern? What would confirm it is a pattern?
