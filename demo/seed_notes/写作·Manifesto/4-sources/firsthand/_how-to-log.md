# 20-evidence — first-hand friction

This folder is the part nobody else writing this manifesto can produce. Credibility comes from either (a) what the author has done, or (b) an argument sharp enough to stand alone. (b) is available now; (a) accrues. This folder is how (a) accrues.

Standing sites: UChicago; Fudan FD-LAMT; SUPERGroup (LLM value inference); the AI music forgery detection honors thesis.

## What to log

Log the friction, not the research. Specifically:

- **Cross-institution:** a collaboration that needed an agreement nobody would sign, or took months to sign. Which office, which clause.
- **Cross-jurisdiction:** data that could not leave a country, a licence that did not survive a border, an IRB or ethics review with no counterpart on the other side, an export-control or PIPL/GDPR question that nobody could answer.
- **Cross-language:** work duplicated because a literature was not read, a term of art that does not translate, a dataset annotated to incompatible standards.
- **Funding shape:** a question dropped because no single panel owned it. Note which panels you considered and why each was wrong. **This is the highest-value category** — it is direct evidence for the partition diagnosis.
- **Credit:** work that had to be structured to be publishable rather than to be right. Authorship disputes on tool-building.
- **Tooling:** infrastructure everyone needed and nobody was funded to build. The FRO thesis in miniature; if you can name three of these from your own experience, you have an article.

## How to log

One incident, one file, `_templates/evidence-note.md`. Write it the day it happens or the week after — reconstructed friction turns into generic complaint, and generic complaint is worthless here.

Aim for **incident report, not reflection**. Dates, offices, clause numbers, weeks lost. A specific number beats a paragraph of adjectives.

## The bar

An entry earns its place if a stranger reading it would say "I did not know that was how it worked." If it reads as "research is bureaucratic," it does not clear the bar.

## The most valuable entries

The ones where the proposed institution **would not have helped**. Those are the notes that change the design instead of decorating it. Flag them.
