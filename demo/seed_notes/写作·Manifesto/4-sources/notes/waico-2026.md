# WAICO (2026)

> **Direct competitor, and the reason "together" is no longer a neutral word.**

## What happened

**Xi Jinping, keynote at the World AI Conference, Shanghai, 17 July 2026**, announcing the **World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization**, headquartered in Shanghai and described by Xinhua as the first intergovernmental AI cooperation organisation.

Four stated principles: openness and innovation; security and controllability; cultural inclusivity; **international solidarity**.

**The Global South offer is concrete, which is the part that matters:** 5,000 training places over five years; regional AI cooperation centres; meteorological-warning access for 30 countries. And the framing — explicit language about preventing *"a new historical injustice in AI."*

## Why this is in the vault

Because it converts the manifesto's centre of gravity into contested geopolitical territory overnight.

- **"AI must benefit all humanity together" is now a great-power instrument**, not a neutral moral claim. It is the declared brand of one state's AI governance strategy, attached to an intergovernmental body and a training budget.
- It sits against a US posture that **rejected the inclusive framing at Paris in February 2025** and **voted against the UN scientific panel's membership in February 2026** (117 in favour; the US and Paraguay opposed).
- So the sentence "humanity should progress together" now has a flag on each side of it.

## Against me

- **Writing "together" in 2026 without addressing this will read as naïve.** Not as wrong — as *unaware*. That is worse.
- **And the offer is not empty.** Training places, regional centres and data access are more concrete mechanisms of distribution than anything in the encyclical, in Amodei, or in the New Delhi Declaration. **The most specific distribution mechanism currently on the table comes attached to a geopolitical project.** A manifesto that wants to own the distribution question has to engage that fact honestly rather than dismiss it.
- **It also demonstrates the opening.** Everyone says "together"; the state that attached a mechanism to it got the attention. That is evidence for opening #1 in `_ai-era-landscape.md`, not against it.

## Implication

**Q3.** WAICO is a live experiment in exactly the binding question — can an intergovernmental AI body create adhesion, and with what asset? Watch what it actually builds. If it constitutes an indivisible shared asset (compute, a model, a dataset under joint governance), that is the first modern candidate for the CERN mechanism and should get its own note.
