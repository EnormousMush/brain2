# The Long Now Foundation

> **The bottleneck.** No institution existed whose *unit of planning* was ten thousand years, and the claim was that the absence of such an institution was itself shortening civilization's horizon.

The closest thing in the vault to "found an institution on a philosophical premise about civilization." Thirty years of evidence about what that produces.

## Facts

Founded **1996** by **Stewart Brand, Danny Hillis, Brian Eno** and others. Nonprofit. Total assets: $5.37M (2011), $9.03M (2014), $5.74M (2018), $6.76M (2020), $10.78M (2022), **$20.58M (2023), $20.46M (2024)** — patronage-driven, with a step change in 2023.

The house style is itself an argument: they write years with five digits — **02026** — a typographic device that makes the premise unavoidable in every sentence.

## The projects, and their honest status in 2026

| project | started | status |
|---|---|---|

Monument site: Van Horn, West Texas, on Bezos land. 500-ft shaft excavated 2011; components assembled underground 2018; **bells rang for the first time in 2022.** No completion date announced |
| **Rosetta Project** | — | **Delivered.** A 1,500-language micro-etched disk, one copy of which is physically on **comet 67P** |
| **Long Bets** | 2002 | **Effectively dead.** Gwern (2017): 29 bets in 15 years, ~1.9/yr falling to 0.8/yr after 2005, "essentially static… no new predictions, no new bets, and not even comments." A 2022 count: 895 predictions, 532 deleted, 17 closed, many years past deadline unresolved |

| **Manual for Civilization** | — | >3,500 crowd-curated books, shelved at The Interval |

## Verdict, and the structural reason

**Mixed, and the pattern is precise: what got built is what could be finished by a small team and displayed; what did not get built is the thing the institution is named for.**

- **The flagship is unbuilt after thirty years.** A clock designed to run ten thousand years has not run one.
- **The falsifiable instrument died.** Long Bets was the one mechanism that would have produced a checkable track record of long-term thinking, and it received about one bet a year before going static. **The institution killed its own scoreboard** — not deliberately, but by neglect, which is the same outcome.
- **The seminar series thrives.** A talks programme with 400+ speakers over twenty-three years is a real cultural artifact and it is also the lowest-commitment activity on the list.
- **The delivered artifacts are objects, not arguments.** The Rosetta Disk on a comet is a genuinely extraordinary thing, and what it demonstrates is craft and patronage rather than any thesis about civilizational time.

**Structurally:** an institution founded on a philosophical premise, with an endowment, patronage, a venue, and no mechanism that forces it to test the premise. Compare `santa-fe-institute.md` — same shape, different field: breadth of purpose, no kill mechanism, no scoreboard, and an output that reads as atmosphere plus a diaspora of good ideas.

## Against me

This is the closest thing to what the manifesto is proposing, and its record should be read carefully rather than warmly.

- **Thirty years, $20M in assets, three famous founders, and the central artifact does not exist.** If the manifesto's institution is founded on an idea rather than on a task, this is the modal outcome — a beautiful venue, an excellent talks programme, and a flagship perpetually under construction.
- **The one falsifiable component atrophied.** That is the specific failure to design against. **Whatever scoreboard the manifesto proposes, assume it will receive one entry a year unless something forces entries.**
- **And yet the *idea* propagated.** "Long now," the 02026 date convention, the pace-layers framing — these are in general circulation and have been for two decades. **The idea travelled and the institution did not.** For a philosophical manifesto that is arguably the correct trade; it should just be made knowingly rather than discovered afterwards.

## Implication

**Q2, and `00-argument/intent.md`.** It sharpens the question the intent file raises: if the institution is the *illustration* of the idea, Long Now shows the illustration can stay unbuilt while the idea works. If the institution is the *ask*, Long Now is a warning about what founding-on-a-premise produces.
