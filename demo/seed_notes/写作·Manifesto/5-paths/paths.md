# Paths — where to go when stuck

**Say "stuck" and this is the file I open.** Each path is a route that does not require solving whatever is blocking you. Ranked by expected value; each says its cost and what it buys.

---

## P1 — Do the audit *(highest value, lowest risk, bounded)*

Ten consensus documents — UNESCO 2021, Bletchley, Seoul, Paris, New Delhi, the Asilomar AI Principles, the Montréal Declaration, *Magnifica Humanitas*, Amodei's essays, Xi's WAICO speech — classified against the five goods in `2-threads/capacity-not-outputs.md`.

**Cost:** a weekend. **Buys:** original evidence, a table for the paper, settlement of C7. Can be done while every conceptual question is still open. **When stuck, do this.**

## P2 — Research one working transfer instrument

**Montreal Protocol Multilateral Fund** first.

**Cost:** a day. **Buys:** an existence proof for §6 — or, if no such instrument exists in any domain, a far stronger and harder essay.

## P3 — Write §7 first

The failure condition, one sentence: what would have to be true in 2031 for this essay to have been wrong.

**Cost:** an hour. **Buys:** it forces the §5 choice and clarifies §6. **The fastest way to make a slippery argument solid.**

## P4 — Switch from arguing to reporting

Write it as **an account of what the consensus documents actually say**, argument emerging from the material.

**Cost:** nothing — same content, different order. **Buys:** it sidesteps "why should I believe this isn't marketing" entirely. **The reframe when the essay starts feeling like a sermon.**

## P5 — Answer "AI as normal technology" head-on

**Cost:** a careful read and a section. **Buys:** possibly the best section. The argument may not need refuting — **a distribution claim is compatible with, arguably strengthened by, AI being normal technology.** Turning the strongest objection into support is the most efficient move available.

## P6 — Go at the agency question instead

**Can a species that concentrates its capacity to produce remain a collective agent at all?** Kulveit et al. wrote the negative version. **The constructive counterpart is unwritten.**

**Cost:** high — a harder essay. **Buys:** the only level-4 contribution currently visible.

## P7 — Write the secular Nehemiah

Babel against Nehemiah's wall, rebuilt on secular ground.

**Cost:** moderate. **Buys:** a whole essay. **Risk:** a borrowed frame; attribute it.

## P8 — Shrink the ask

Russell–Einstein asked only that *scientists should assemble in conference*; one man financed it four days later. **Size the ask so one person can say yes.** If §6 will not resolve into a mechanism: not "here is the instrument" but "here is the one meeting, dataset, audit or comparison that does not exist and should."

---

## When genuinely blocked, in order

1. Write §7. One sentence. *(an hour)*
2. Do the audit. *(a weekend)*
3. Log one first-hand incident. *(twenty minutes, and it is the only irreplaceable material)*
