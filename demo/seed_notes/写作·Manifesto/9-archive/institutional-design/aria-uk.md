# ARIA (UK)

> **The bottleneck.** A UK public funder that can make concentrated high-variance bets — and **found new institutions** — without peer-review consensus or FOI-driven risk aversion.

The live experiment in "broad at the top, narrow at the bottom," and the funder that made the FRO model transnational.

## Facts

**Advanced Research and Invention Agency Act 2022**; formally established **January 2023** as a non-departmental public body with a **statutory 10-year term**. Intellectual origin: Dominic Cummings' proposal. **£800M** initial; **minimum £1bn over the 2025–2029 spending review period**.

**The programme director model.** PDs define their own opportunity spaces and hold real allocation authority. Cohort 1: 8 PDs, October 2023. Cohort 2: 8 PDs, April 2025. Cohort 3 applications open **August 2026**, starting May 2027. Chair Matt Clifford. Founding CEO **Ilan Gur announced his departure in June 2025**; **Kathleen Fisher** was named CEO-elect in November 2025 and took over in **February 2026**. Deputy CEO Pippy James.

**Thirteen active opportunity spaces (one paused):** Sculpting Innate Immunity; Scalable Neural Interfaces; Bioenergetic Engineering; Extending Our Perception; Trust Everything, Everywhere; Mathematics for Safe AI; Nature Computes Better *(paused)*; Collective Flourishing; Scoping Our Planet; Programmable Plants; Engineering Ecosystem Resilience; Future Proofing Our Climate and Weather; Smarter Robot Bodies; Manufacturing Abundance.

Framing of what it is for: problems "too applied for academia, too field-wide in scope for industry, or too complex for fragmented funding."

## Scope: how wide, and what holds it there

**Deliberately broad at agency level and narrow per programme director — the inverse of the FRO approach, and the two composed cleanly** when ARIA used Convergent's residency to launch **Meridial (£14M)** and **Echo Labs (£7M)** in April 2026 under two of its own opportunity spaces.

Enforcement lives entirely with the PD. There is no central mandate constraining what ARIA may work on; there is a person with a budget, a defended thesis, and a term.

## Kill mechanism

Two, at different levels: the PD's term structure ends programmes, and the **agency itself has a statutory 10-year term** — an institution designed with an expiry date, which is rare enough to be worth noting on its own. Compare the FRO clock at organisation scale.

## Verdict, and the structural reason

**Too early.** Track record so far: a £50M geoengineering programme (April 2025); NHS brain-computer interface trials via Forest Neurotech; £6.7M to Oxford for GM potato and wheat yields; Forecasting Tipping Points with 27 international teams; Scaling Compute, targeting a 1,000× AI cost reduction; the two UK FROs.

**The structural question it has not answered is the DARPA-clone question** (see `darpa.md`): DARPA's convergence device is a customer with a ~$190B procurement budget who will scale a successful prototype. ARIA has no such buyer. Whether the PD model works without one is precisely what is being tested.

## Against me

- **Public money crossing borders draws fire.** A 2026 Guardian report: **£23M to nine US tech companies and £29.4M to three US venture capital groups**, with Labour MP Dawn Butler raising taxpayer-oversight concerns. Any institution proposing to be transnational with public funding should expect this exact objection, and should have an answer that is not "science is universal."
- **Opacity is part of the design and it costs.** ARIA is **statutorily exempt from Freedom of Information**; an attempt to repeal the exemption was defeated in Parliament. The insulation that makes high-variance funding possible is the same insulation critics call unaccountable. Choose deliberately and defend it in the text.
- **Its riskiest programme drew an international-law objection.** Scientists warned of disrupted rainfall from the geoengineering programme, and ETC Group accused ARIA of "legitimising outdoor geoengineering" contrary to the UN Convention on Biological Diversity. A "hardest problems facing humanity" institution will hit this class of objection immediately, and it is a governance problem, not a science problem.

## Implication

**Q2, answer-shape 3.** ARIA is the strongest existing proof that "one roof, many problems" need not mean "one broad mandate." The unit is the programme director, not the department. If the manifesto adopts this, the ten departments become sixteen people with budgets and terms — and the document's real content becomes *how those people are chosen and what ends their programmes*.
