# Intent

**Read before anything else. Everything in this vault is subordinate to this file.**

## Stated by Frank, verbatim

> 这个 manifesto 我想写的还是更偏哲学一点，偏思想一点，更多的是想应景在这个 AI 快速发展的年代，我们如何作为一个人类文明一起进步。

## In English

A **philosophical** work — of ideas, not administration — situated in an age of rapid AI development, about **how we advance together as one human civilization.**

## Three things this fixes

1. **The genre is philosophical.** It argues about what we owe each other and what kind of progress is worth having. It is not a funding proposal or an org design.
2. **The moment is the AI transition.** It is *of* this decade. It addresses a civilization being reorganised by a technology while it argues about it.
3. **The subject is "together."** Not "how does research get done faster," but **how a species fragmenting — by nation, by language, by access to compute, by who owns the models — makes progress as a single thing.**

## What this does not excuse

Philosophical is not permission to be vague. It **raises** the originality bar, because the space of "AI is changing everything, we must proceed wisely together" documents is, as of 2026, extremely crowded — see `4-sources/notes/_ai-era-landscape.md`.

A philosophical piece in a crowded space with no sharp diagnosis does not get argued with. It gets skimmed.

## The standing test

**What would have to be true in the world for this document to be wrong?**

Keep an answer at all times. `3-argument/claims.md` holds it.
