# Thread — fragmentation, observed

**The vault's biggest structural weakness: three conceptual threads and one empirical, and this is the empty one.**

Nobody else writing in this space has worked inside research systems on both sides of the US–China divide. That is not a credential — it is *evidence*, and the only evidence in this project another author cannot replicate.

## What belongs here

Not opinions. **Incidents.**

- A collaboration that needed an agreement nobody would sign. Which office, which clause, how many weeks.
- Data that could not cross a border. Which rule.
- An ethics or IRB review with no counterpart on the other side.
- A literature not read because of language, and what was duplicated as a result.
- A term of art that does not translate, and what broke.
- Compute or model access available on one side and not the other, and what work that made impossible.
- A question dropped because no single panel owned it.

## The bar

A stranger reads it and says **"I didn't know that was how it worked."**

## Why it matters more in a philosophical essay than a policy one

§3 needs a claim about how the *nature* of distribution has changed. Argued abstractly, it competes with professional philosophers. **Grounded in something seen, it is a position only you hold.**
