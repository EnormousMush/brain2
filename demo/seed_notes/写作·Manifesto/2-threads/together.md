# Thread — "together"

The word doing the most work in `INTENT.md`, and the least examined.

## The four senses

**1. Benefits reach everyone.** Progress whose *outputs* are universally available.
- Distributes: outputs, access. Claimed by: everyone (see landscape §D, claim 3).
- Failure looks like: a diagnostic model that works in Boston and is unavailable in Lagos.
- **Weakness: the saturated sense. Choosing it means competing with a claim 193 states have made.**

**2. Everyone participates in production.** Progress whose *making* is not confined to a few places.
- Distributes: capability, agenda-setting. Claimed by: almost nobody; WAICO's training places gesture at it.
- Failure looks like: thirty-two countries with data centres and a hundred and fifty without, permanently.
- **Strength: the only sense that requires capacity rather than outputs, and the one the consensus does not claim. Current recommendation.**

**3. The direction is agreed.** Progress whose *ends* a fragmented species can converge on.
- Distributes: the standing to set the agenda. Claimed by: the summit series, implicitly.
- Failure looks like: two blocs, two institutions, no shared object.
- **Weakness: it may simply be false that a fragmented species can converge, and assuming it is the failure mode this vault exists to prevent.**

**4. Nobody is left behind.** Progress that does not proceed *by means of* abandoning part of humanity.
- Distributes: the standing to refuse. Claimed by: Hao (as critique), the encyclical (as principle).
- Failure looks like: real aggregate gains purchased from a population that did not consent.
- **Strength: strongest moral claim. Weakness: closest to occupied ground.**

## Frames available

- **Jonas's parent–child paradigm** — asymmetric, non-reciprocal, unchosen responsibility triggered by vulnerability. **The strongest answer to "why do we owe anything to those who cannot reciprocate"** — the hard part of senses 1 and 4. → `4-sources/notes/jonas-1979-imperative-of-responsibility.md`
- **The Russell–Einstein substitution** — a biological category replacing a political one: *"members of the species Man."* The cleanest move for making "humanity" a speaking position. → `4-sources/notes/russell-einstein-manifesto.md`
- **Babel versus Nehemiah** — concentrated homogenised building vs distributed, community-accountable building. Locked in a religious document; the secular translation is an opening. → `4-sources/notes/magnifica-humanitas-2026.md`
- **Gradual disempowerment** (Kulveit et al., 2025) — humanity ceasing to function as a collective agent, framed entirely as loss. **The constructive counterpart has not been written.** Where senses 2 and 3 meet.
