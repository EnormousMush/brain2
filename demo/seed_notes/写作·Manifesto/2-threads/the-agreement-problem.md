# Thread — the agreement problem

## The claim

Universal agreement on a distributive aspiration is not a step toward achieving it, and may be what prevents it. A proposition endorsed by everyone with power has no addressee. It cannot be violated and cannot be met. It converts a question about instruments into a question about sentiment.

## Why it is more than cynicism

The cynical version — "they don't mean it" — is **weaker and probably false**. Amodei means it, the Pope means it, most of the 193 states mean it.

The structural version: **sincerity is irrelevant to the outcome, because the aspiration performs the moral work a mechanism would otherwise have to perform.** A claim about how consensus functions, not about character. That is the version worth defending.

## The prediction that would make it a diagnosis

If the consensus is stabilising rather than mobilising, these documents should become **more universal and less specific over time**. Checkable against the 2017–2026 corpus — see `3-argument/seams.md` S3.
