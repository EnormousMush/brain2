## The one-line form

A non-profit institution whose sole purpose is research in service of all humanity, on the hardest problems humanity faces or will face; its foundation is not a building but **a network of unreserved sharing**; its resources come from **beyond Earth**, so it owes nothing to any state or any capital.

## Mission and stance

- Human-centered; crosses all boundaries of politics, ethnicity, and difference.
- Non-profit: pays workers and employees; the majority of surplus returns to research.
- The value commitment: humanity and civilization above all. *(⊕ reformulated per pushback accepted [phone]: the filter is the **sharpness of the mission**, not a professed creed — Arc and the FROs found that a mission stated sharply enough does the selection by itself. ## The intergalactic premise

Institutions like this were never built **because of resource scarcity** — every large resource on Earth already has an owner, and taking anyone's money means owing them. So: extract resources from space first, and build with wealth that has no owner. *(Detail wave coming — Frank, next batch.)*

⊕ **The legal foundation this instinct already has.** The Outer Space Treaty (in force 10 October 1967; 118 parties including every major spacefaring state) declares in Article I that the exploration and use of outer space "shall be the province of all mankind," and Article II forbids national appropriation of celestial bodies "by claim of sovereignty, by means of use or occupation, or by any other means." **So an institution built from space resources and serving all humanity is not doing charity — it is returning property that the law already assigns to everyone.** This upgrades "intergalactic" from a sci-fi flourish to a legal-philosophical argument.

⊕ **The complication, which is also an opportunity:** the Moon Agreement's stronger "common heritage of mankind" clause has only 18 parties — the US, Russia, China and India never ratified — and the **US Commercial Space Launch Competitiveness Act (2015)** and **Luxembourg's 2017 law** unilaterally legalised private ownership of extracted space resources, in contested tension with Article II. **The commons is being enclosed right now, in real time.** That is an obstacle to the funding model — and it is *exactly* the kind of unrepaired legal gap the Law & Universal Justice branch exists for. The institution's first legal case is its own foundation.

## The network

Built on universities (labs, professors, students, clubs) and education generally; connects companies, institutions and individuals worldwide; must reach the people — the citizens of Earth — fast. Example: a medical breakthrough should reach all hospitals in the world quickly. The foundation of the institution **is** the network; the key is **to share, and to share without reserve.**

⊕ Two distinct pipes are being described, and keeping them distinct matters (`DRAFT.md` §4):
- the **university network** distributes *capacity* — it grows people who can produce;
- the **hospital pipe** distributes *outputs* — results, protocols.
This is the first design in the vault that claims both pipes at once.

⊕ **The precedent for "share without reserve" as a founding rule:** the **Bermuda Principles (1996)** — Human Genome Project leaders agreed that all DNA sequence assemblies >1kb be released to public databases **within 24 hours of generation**, before publication, unconditionally, the whole sequence in the public domain. It contravened normal scientific practice and it held. **A sharing rule, voluntarily adopted, became the constitution of a field.** This is the network's prototype and it should be studied before the network is specified further.

## Governance

- **Branch leaders:** one decision-maker per branch; succession decided **entirely by vote within the branch**. Criterion in Frank's words: the most leader-like, the readiest to serve, "the one that would be willing to die for the people." *(⊕ mechanised per pushback accepted [phone]: bounded term, no equity, structurally unable to profit from the seat, exit into service rather than wealth — "willing to die for the people" translated into verifiable structure. Original phrasing preserved in inbox #002.)*
- **Overall leader:** rotates on a **10–20 year period**, kept young, connects the branches, makes cross-branch decisions.
- **Administration:** directly under the overall leader — finance, operations, marketing — and responsible for ensuring the branches actually collaborate.

⊕ The nearest working relative is DARPA's term structure, and its lesson is worth writing in: **rotation's deepest function is not youth — it is that a bounded leader can afford to end things**, because their career does not depend on any programme surviving.
