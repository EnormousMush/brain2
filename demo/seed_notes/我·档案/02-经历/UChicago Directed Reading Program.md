## 报告摘要（英文原文，现成可复用）

你为这场报告写的正式摘要（[phone] 入档；slides PDF 同步在档，与网站挂的 drp.pdf 为同一份）：

> This presentation provides a foundational introduction to elliptic curves as a precursor to the Elliptic Curve Primality Test. Beginning with the language of algebraic geometry, we review affine and projective varieties, Hilbert's Nullstellensatz, and the notion of smoothness for algebraic curves. These tools are then used to define elliptic curves rigorously as smooth projective curves of genus one equipped with a distinguished point. We examine elliptic curves in the projective plane via the short Weierstrass equation, emphasizing the role of nonsingularity and the interpretation of points at infinity. Finally, we introduce the group law on elliptic curves, describing its geometric construction and key algebraic properties. This overview establishes the conceptual and geometric framework necessary for later developments, including morphisms of curves, Hasse's theorem, and the Goldwasser-Kilian primality testing algorithm.
